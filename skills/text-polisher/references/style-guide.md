# Text Polisher Style Guide

Use these style targets only when the user requests them.

## Formal

Prefer complete sentences, specific verbs, and restrained phrasing. Avoid slang, contractions, and overly casual openings.

## Friendly

Keep the wording warm and natural. Use contractions where appropriate, but avoid exaggerated enthusiasm.

## Concise

Remove throat-clearing, repeated ideas, unnecessary intensifiers, and soft qualifiers. Keep the strongest sentence structure that preserves meaning.

## Assertive

Lead with the request, decision, or recommendation. Remove unnecessary apologies and hedging while keeping the tone respectful.

## Diplomatic

Keep disagreement or correction clear, but frame it around shared goals, observable facts, and next steps.
